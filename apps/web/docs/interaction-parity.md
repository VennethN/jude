## Local verification and external sign-off

- Synthetic cases resolve labelled local Pasal.id examples. A saved legal reference opens its article metadata separately from the numbered case evidence. Private discovery uses a server-side Pasal.id token and sends the normalized public citation. SQLite and PostgreSQL tests used mocked upstream responses to verify case isolation, caching and fallback on a missing token, rate limit or outage. Uncited regulation text in testimony triggered zero provider requests. A credentialed upstream request remains open. [Legal discovery UI](../tests/e2e/legal-discovery.spec.ts) · [Provider checks](../../../packages/core/tests/pasal.test.ts)
- Chrome’s first submitted microphone turn contained **2.4 seconds** of continuous test speech at 24 kHz. The local WebSocket fixture showed its preview after about 2.6 seconds. End-to-end OpenAI latency and diarization with provisioned model weights still require a credentialed recording. [Microphone checks](../tests/e2e/microphone.spec.ts)
- These desktop and phone results came from local Jude builds. Production sign-off still requires a deployed AWS health check. Email delivery and payment collection require configured services and end-to-end checks. [EC2 deployment checks](aws.md)
