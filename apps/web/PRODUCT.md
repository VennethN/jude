## Platform

Web, in Bahasa Indonesia and English. Source records stay in their original language.

## Interface language and source wording

All product-owned navigation, labels, explanations, status and error messages, and generated question drafts follow the selected Indonesian or English interface language. Synthetic examples carry translations for their display labels and suggested questions, including examples already saved.

The public landing story also translates its synthetic illustration text, transcript excerpts and paper facsimiles into the selected language. Mark English illustrations as translations and link to the unchanged Indonesian case records. Preserve names, official identifiers and the original document’s Latin heading. These public display translations never replace evidence in the case workspace.

Keep verbatim testimony, original document text and scans, official legal identifiers (BAP, KUHP, KUHAP, article/case numbers), proper names, and user-authored titles, notes and messages unchanged. These are source or authored records, not interface copy. Explain this boundary beside evidence and in language preferences. Never silently translate or overwrite evidence. Saved courtroom drafts without a verified translation remain explicitly identified as original wording. Copying a translated suggestion copies the language shown.

## Users

Counsel preparing Indonesian criminal cases and following testimony in a hearing. A lawyer may have only a few seconds to find the passage behind a question.

## Product purpose

Connect what was said in court to what the case file records. A lawyer can read both phrases, inspect the originals and decide what to ask.

## Operating context

A laptop on the counsel table, a tablet in the hearing room, or a phone between hearings. Transcripts, citation anchors and reading positions have to survive navigation.

## Brand commitments

Quiet type and generous reading space. Written records use a typewriter face, testimony a humanist sans, and questions an editorial serif. Colour always has an accompanying label.

## Evidence on hand

A fictional warehouse case includes hearing lines, exhibits and marked passages. Keep every demonstration visibly synthetic.

## Product principles

Keep the lawyer in control of recording, reviewing findings and interpreting evidence. Check the current case on every read and write. Store the original document separately from its extracted text. Make the source reachable from the claim.

## Open decisions

Paid checkout, provider accounts, commercial prices, retention periods and regional processing commitments require an owner decision before launch.
