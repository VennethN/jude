# The open case file

The lawyer arrives to review a discrepancy, prepare the next question and get back to the hearing. The most important comparison occupies the first reading surface. The rail describes places, the index describes records, and every source carries a citation. On a phone, the record becomes a single reading column with persistent case navigation.
